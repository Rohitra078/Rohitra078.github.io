02 // Background

Multidisciplinary by nature. Roboticist by passion.

I am a Robotics & AI Engineer who thrives at the intersection of mechanical design, embedded compute, and modern perception. I hold a Master’s in Robotics Engineering from Northeastern University, where my thesis work focused on enabling the M4 (Multi-Modal Mobility Morphobot) robot to autonomously choose its locomotion mode and navigate complex Martian-like terrain.

With a foundational B.E. in Mechanical Engineering, I previously worked as a design engineer at Faurecia and co-founded Tectural Technology as Head of the Robotics Division. This blend gives me end-to-end perspective: I design physical mechanisms in CAD, instrument them with sensors, and write the perception, SLAM, and planning software that breathes autonomous life into them.

My daily stack includes Python, C++, ROS / ROS 2, PyTorch, Linux, Gazebo, and hands-on sensor integration with LiDAR, depth cameras, and RTOS flight controllers.
